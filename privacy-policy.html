LS Duet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LS Duet ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Best-IT, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the LS Duet mobile application and related services.

By using LS Duet you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

  • App name: LS Duet
  • Provider: Best-IT
  • Registered address: 1 Hatachana st. Kfar Saba, Israel
  • Contact for privacy questions: info@lifesync-connect.com

LS Duet is a focused WhatsApp companion app that provides WhatsApp conversations, per-contact automatic translation, scheduled message sending, contacts management, a personal knowledge base, and tasks & reminders.

2. Summary (the short version)

  • Most of your content — your message history, contacts, tasks, reminders, and knowledge items — is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • A limited set of data must reach our servers to power server-dependent features: your account identity, WhatsApp session state, translation settings, scheduled messages, and push-notification tokens.
  • To translate messages, message text is sent to our translation providers (Anthropic and Google) at the moment of translation.
  • We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.
  • The App is intended for adults (18+) and business/personal messaging use.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information

When you sign in (via Google, Apple, or email/password through Firebase Authentication) we process:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name (if provided by the sign-in provider)
  • A unique account identifier (Firebase UID) and your authentication provider ID

3.2 WhatsApp data

LS Duet connects to your WhatsApp account (through the WAHA WhatsApp gateway). Depending on your use, this involves:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the session/pairing credentials needed to keep your WhatsApp linked.
  • Message content — the text of messages you send and receive. Incoming and outgoing message history is stored locally on your device. A transient copy of relevant messages passes through our servers to enable delivery, translation, and (for scheduled messages) timed sending.
  • Contacts and group information from your WhatsApp — names, phone numbers, group names, and identifier mappings (LID mappings) used to resolve who a message is from.

3.3 Content you create in the App

  • Scheduled messages (recipient, content, and send time) — stored on our servers because timed delivery requires a server.
  • Per-contact translation settings (which language to translate to/from) — stored on our servers so the backend can auto-translate.
  • Tasks, reminders, and knowledge-base items — stored locally on your device only.

3.4 Device permissions

  • Contacts — with your permission, the App can import contacts from your device to make them available inside the App. Imported contacts are stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications — the App registers a push-notification token (via Expo / the platform push service) so we can notify you of new messages. This token, and your notifications on/off preference, are stored on our servers to deliver pushes.

3.5 Technical and usage data

  • Basic operational data needed to run the service (e.g. WhatsApp session status, API request counts, and aggregate translation/traffic counters used for internal service monitoring and usage reporting).
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

We do not knowingly collect precise location, biometric data, financial account data, or health data.

4. How we use your information

We use the information above solely to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • Send, receive, schedule, and display your WhatsApp messages.
  • Automatically translate messages according to your settings.
  • Resolve contacts and group identities so conversations display correctly.
  • Deliver push notifications you have enabled.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and monitor the reliability of the service.
  • Provide you (and, where applicable, the account administrator) with usage summaries.

We process this data to perform the service you request and to pursue our legitimate interest in operating a reliable, secure product.

5. Automatic translation — message content and AI providers

When translation is enabled for a conversation, the text of the relevant message is transmitted to our translation providers to produce the translated text:

  • Anthropic (Claude models) — primary translation engine.
  • Google Cloud Translation — fallback engine.

Only the message text (and the source/target language) needed to perform the translation is sent. These providers act as data processors for the sole purpose of returning a translation. We do not permit them to use your content to train their models beyond what their enterprise/API terms allow, and no translated content is used by us for advertising.

If you do not want message content sent for translation, disable translation for that contact/conversation in the App's settings.

6. Where your data is stored

  • On your device (local SQLite database): contacts, full message history, tasks, reminders, and knowledge items. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • On our servers (Google Firebase / Firestore, region europe-west3): account identity, WhatsApp session state, translation settings, scheduled messages, identifier (LID) mappings, push tokens, and operational counters.

Message documents that transit our Firestore backend are retained only briefly (a short rolling window, on the order of ~48 hours) and are then automatically deleted by a scheduled cleanup job. Scheduled messages are retained until they are sent (or cancelled).

8. How we share information

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing.
  • We share data only with the service providers listed above (as processors), and only as needed to run the service.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and service.

9. Data retention

  • Local data (messages, contacts, tasks, reminders, knowledge) persists on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Transient message documents on our servers are auto-deleted within a short rolling window (~48 hours).
  • Account, session, translation settings, scheduled messages, and push tokens are retained for as long as your account is active.
  • When you close your account (see Section 10), your server-side account data is deleted.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: You can view and edit your content within the App.
  • Contacts permission: You can decline or later revoke the contacts permission in your device settings; the App remains usable with reduced functionality.
  • Notifications: You can turn push notifications off in the App's settings or in your device settings.
  • Translation: You can disable translation per conversation at any time.
  • Account deletion: You can request deletion of your account and all associated server-side data at any time. See our dedicated Account Deletion page for the steps and details of what is deleted. Data stored locally on your device is removed by uninstalling the App.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/EEA, UK), you may have additional rights under the GDPR or similar laws — including access, deletion, restriction, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address in Section 1.

11. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for all network communication, authenticated access to backend resources, and server-only access controls on sensitive collections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and limit access to it.

12. Children's privacy

LS Duet is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International data transfers

Our backend is hosted in Google Cloud (region europe-west3, in the EU). Some service providers (e.g. Anthropic, Google, Expo, Resend) may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where required, such transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).
