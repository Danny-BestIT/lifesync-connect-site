# Delete your LS Duet account **App:** LS Duet **Developer:** Best-IT **Last updated:** July 8, 2026 This page explains how to request deletion of your **LS Duet** account and the personal data associated with it, what data is deleted, what (if anything) is retained, and for how long. --- ## How to request account deletion You can request that your LS Duet account and its associated data be deleted in either of these ways: 1. **Email us** — send a request from the email address associated with your LS Duet account to **info@lifesync-connect.com** with the subject line **"Delete my account"**. Sending from your account email lets us verify the request. If you can only write from another address, include the account email in the message. 2. **From within the app** — open **Settings** and use the account/logout area to contact us for closure. *(A one-tap in-app "Delete account" button is being added; until then, use the email method above, which we action promptly.)* We will verify the request and complete the deletion. You will receive a confirmation once it is done. --- ## What gets deleted When your account is deleted, we permanently remove all of the following server-side data: - Your **account / sign-in identity** (Firebase Authentication user — email, display name, and account identifier). - Your **WhatsApp session and pairing state**, and your session is unlinked. - **Message data** associated with your account or session stored on our servers. - Your **per-contact translation settings**. - Your **scheduled messages**. - Your **push-notification tokens** and notification preferences. - Your entire **user profile record** on our servers (the `users/{account}` data tree). **Data on your device:** Your local content (message history, contacts, tasks, reminders, and knowledge items) is stored only on your device. It is removed when you **uninstall the app** or clear the app's data — deleting your account does not by itself erase the copy held locally on your device. --- ## What is retained, and for how long - For security, audit, and abuse-prevention purposes, we retain a **minimal record that the account was closed** (the account email address and the closure date). This record contains no message content, contacts, or profile data. - **Transient message documents** that pass through our servers are automatically deleted within a short rolling window (on the order of ~48 hours) as part of normal operation. - Deletion requests are actioned **within 30 days** of a verified request; in practice this is usually completed much sooner. --- ## Questions If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at **info@lifesync-connect.com**. See also our [Privacy Policy](https://lifesync-connect.com/privacy-policy).
